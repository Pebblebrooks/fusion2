The four accounts

Your CPF savings sit in up to four accounts. Each has its own purpose and interest rate.

AccountWhat it's forInterest
Ordinary (OA)Housing, approved investments and education. Remains open for life.2.5%
Special (SA)Retirement savings before 55. Closed at 55 since January 2025 — the balance moves to your RA, and anything above your Full Retirement Sum moves to your OA.4.0% floor
MediSave (MA)Hospital bills, approved medical insurance and treatments. Capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum; contributions above the cap flow to your SA or RA (or OA once those are full).4.0% floor
Retirement (RA)Created at 55 from your SA and OA savings. It funds your CPF LIFE monthly payouts.4.0% floor

Extra interest

On top of the base rates, CPF pays extra interest on your first S$60,000 of combined balances (of which no more than S$20,000 can come from your OA):

  • Below 55: an extra 1% on the first S$60,000.
  • 55 and above: an extra 2% on the first S$30,000, and an extra 1% on the next S$30,000.

Extra interest earned because of your OA balance is paid into your SA or RA, not your OA.

CPF LIFE

CPF LIFE turns your RA into a monthly income for as long as you live. If you were born in 1958 or later and have at least S$60,000 in your RA, you are included automatically. Payouts can start from 65.

The three plans

  • Standard — level monthly payouts for life. This is the default.
  • Escalating — starts lower, then rises 2% every year to help keep pace with prices.
  • Basic — lower monthly payouts but more left for your beneficiaries, because your RA is drawn down gradually before the annuity takes over.

Deferring payouts

You can start payouts as late as 70. Each year you defer raises your monthly payout by up to 7%, because your RA keeps earning interest and the payouts cover fewer years.

Trade-off: deferring pays more each month but less in total if you die early. Fusion's Payout Plans view shows the break-even age for each choice.

The 2026 playbook

Moves worth knowing about. None is right for everyone — most lock money away for good, so check your cash needs first.

Before 55

Top up your SA while it's open

Cash top-ups to your SA (RSTU) earn the 4% floor plus extra interest. Your SA closes at 55, so earlier top-ups compound for longer. Tax relief of up to S$8,000 a year.

Tax relief

Top up family members too

Top-ups to a spouse's, parent's, grandparent's or sibling's SA or RA qualify for a separate relief of up to S$8,000 — up to S$16,000 a year in total.

Dollar for dollar

Matched Retirement Savings Scheme

For eligible Singapore Citizens aged 55 and above with less than the current BRS in their RA, the Government matches cash top-ups up to S$2,000 a year (lifetime cap S$20,000). Check eligibility with CPF.

≈ S$16,500

OA to SA or RA transfers

S$30,000 left in the OA for 20 years earns about S$16,500 less than at 4%. But transfers can't be reversed, and that money can no longer pay for housing.

Up to 7% a year

Defer CPF LIFE

Each year you delay payouts past 65 (up to 70) raises your monthly income. Best if you have other income to bridge the gap.

At 55

Aim beyond the FRS

You can top up your RA to the Enhanced Retirement Sum (S$440,800 for those turning 55 in 2026) for higher lifelong payouts.

Housing

Voluntary housing refund

OA money used for housing must be refunded with 2.5% accrued interest when you sell. Refunding early puts the money back to work in your CPF sooner.

Tax

Supplementary Retirement Scheme

Contribute up to S$15,300 a year (S$35,700 for foreigners) and deduct it from taxable income. Only 50% of withdrawals are taxed if you withdraw from your SRS retirement age (63, or 64 if your first contribution was made from 1 July 2026), spread over up to 10 years.

Timing

Top up in January

CPF interest is calculated on each month's lowest balance. A top-up made early in the year earns interest for more months than the same top-up in December.

After 65

Unlock home equity

The Lease Buyback Scheme and the Silver Housing Bonus (a cash bonus of up to S$30,000 for eligible households who right-size and top up their RA) can raise retirement income without a full sale.

Rules and eligibility change. Always confirm with CPF Board and IRAS before acting.

Protecting your CPF

Scammers target CPF savings, especially around 55 and 65. CPF offers several safeguards you can turn on yourself:

  • CPF Withdrawal Lock — blocks online withdrawals while keeping your online access to view balances.
  • Safety Switch — for members 55 and above, an emergency brake that disables online access and withdrawals if you suspect a scam.
  • Trusted Contact — nominate someone to be alerted to major transactions, without giving them access to your account.

How Fusion models this

  • Contributions use the rate table for your age, citizenship and PR year, capped at the wage ceilings and the Annual Limit.
  • Interest is credited yearly at the base rates plus extra interest, assuming the 4% floor continues unless you change it in a stress test.
  • Future retirement sums grow at a rate you choose (3% a year by default), starting from the published figures.
  • CPF LIFE payouts are estimated from CPF Board payout tables for your RA balance, plan and payout age.
  • Every year of the projection is visible in the Data Table, so you can check any number.

Projections are estimates, not guarantees. Actual payouts are set by CPF Board when you start them.
