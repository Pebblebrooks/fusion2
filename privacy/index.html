Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short: your planning inputs — salary, CPF balances, ages and plans — are processed in your browser and stored on your device. We don't receive them. We only collect information you choose to send us, such as a message or a payment.

1. Planning data stays on your device

The Fusion planner runs in your web browser. Calculations happen on your device, and your inputs are saved in your browser's local storage so your plan is there next time. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes them; use Export Profile to keep a backup.

If you create a share link, your plan is encoded into the link. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it only with people you trust.

2. Information you send us

  • Messages and access requests. When you use the contact form or request beta access, we collect your name (optional), email address and message. These are stored in a private Google Sheet that only the developer can access, and used only to reply to you.
  • Plan requests and payments. When you choose a paid plan, we collect your email address, plan and billing choice. Payments are processed by Stripe, which receives your payment details directly; we never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of your plan and payment status so we can give you access, send receipts and handle renewals.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage for your plan and interface preferences (such as light or dark mode). We use a small number of cookies only to keep you signed in and to remember your plan access. We do not use advertising cookies, and our fonts are served from our own site.

4. Hosting and visit statistics

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies or local storage, does not track you across other websites, and does not record the part of a web address after “?”, which is where share links carry plan data. Your planning inputs are never sent to it. We see only totals, such as how many people visited a page.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with the service providers named above (Google, Stripe and Cloudflare) to run the service, or where the law requires.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep messages for as long as needed to respond, and billing records for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012.
