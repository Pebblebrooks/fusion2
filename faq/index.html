About Fusion

Is Fusion affiliated with the CPF Board?

No. Fusion 富生 is an independent educational tool. It is not supported, approved or endorsed by the CPF Board or any government agency. For official figures, use your CPF account at cpf.gov.sg.

Is this financial advice?

No. Fusion shows estimates based on the inputs and assumptions you give it and on published CPF rules. It does not recommend specific products. For advice on your situation, speak to a licensed financial adviser.

Why do I need an access code?

Fusion is in private beta while we finish accounts and payments. Request an access code and we'll email you one.

Are the CPF rates and limits up to date?

Yes. The engine uses the 2026 contribution rates, OA interest of 2.5%, the 4% floor for SA, MA and RA (extended to 31 December 2027), and the 2026 retirement sums: BRS S$110,200, FRS S$220,400, ERS S$440,800 and BHS S$79,000. See Learn CPF for the full list.

Privacy and your data

Is my financial data saved anywhere?

Your salary, balances and other planning inputs are processed in your own browser and saved only on your device. They are not sent to our servers.

If you create a share link, your plan is encoded into the link itself — anyone you send it to can open it, so share it only with people you trust.

Do I need to log in with Singpass?

No. Fusion never asks for Singpass, your CPF password or one-time codes. You type in your own balances, which you can find in your CPF account.

How do I keep a backup of my plan?

Use Export Profile in the planner to save a file you can import later or on another device.

Plans and billing

What's free?

The Guided plan is free: your CPF projection, readiness score, CPF LIFE estimate, what-if sliders and warnings. Compare plans.

What do Workspace and Adviser add?

Workspace adds the full planning toolkit — strategy tools, stress tests, Monte Carlo, life events, CPF LIFE plan comparison, household planning and year-by-year data. Adviser adds a licence for client work and priority support, with a client workspace in development.

Can I pay with PayNow?

Yes, for yearly billing. Monthly billing is by card.

What the planner models

Does it support Permanent Residents?

Yes. Choose PR and your PR year. Fusion applies the graduated (G/G) or full-employer (F/G) rates for years one and two, then moves to full rates automatically as the projection runs.

Does it support self-employed people?

Yes. In self-employed mode, enter your Net Trade Income. Fusion applies the MediSave contribution rates for your income band and shows how much room you have for voluntary contributions.

What if I was born before 1958?

You are likely on the Retirement Sum Scheme rather than CPF LIFE. Fusion has a separate RSS projection that shows how long your monthly payouts will last.

Which CPF LIFE plan should I choose?

It depends on whether you value a higher monthly income, protection against rising prices or more left for your family. Fusion compares Standard, Escalating and Basic side by side, including the break-even ages. How the plans work.

What stress tests are there?

Two kinds. The Stress Centre runs named scenarios — such as a CPF rate cut, an inflation shock, a property correction, a healthcare cost surge and a sequence-of-returns crash — over your base plan. Monte Carlo runs 500 simulations with varied interest rates and shows the P10–P90 range of outcomes.

What are life events?

Real-world changes you can add to your plan: retrenchment, a career break, a medical crisis, a windfall, early retirement, high inflation, a salary cut, loan repayments, charitable giving and part-time work in semi-retirement (Barista FIRE). Each has its own start and end age and amount.

What's the difference between the Goal Simulator and the Turbocharger?

The Goal Simulator works backwards: you enter the CPF balance you want and it works out the yearly top-ups needed. The Turbocharger works forwards: it scans the levers available to you today and shows what each would add.

Can I plan as a couple?

Yes. Household mode adds a spouse or partner with their own balances, salary and CPF LIFE settings, and shows your combined retirement income.

What if something goes wrong in the planner?

If the planner hits an unexpected error, it shows a recovery screen with a reload button. Your inputs stay on your device. Please report the problem so we can fix it.
