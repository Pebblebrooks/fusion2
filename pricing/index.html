Billing questions

Can I pay with PayNow?

Yes, on yearly billing. PayNow payments can't renew automatically, so we email you a PayNow invoice before each renewal. Monthly billing is by card only.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, then returns to Guided. Your saved plans are not deleted.

How does the Adviser trial work?

You get full Adviser access for 14 days. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, your first payment is taken then. We remind you by email three days before.

Do you offer seats for a team?

Yes. Adviser is priced per seat. For five or more seats, contact us for invoicing.

Is GST included?

Prices shown are what you pay. If GST becomes chargeable, it will be shown at checkout before you pay.
